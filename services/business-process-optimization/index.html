Business Process Optimization & Advisory FAQs

What is business process optimization?

Business process optimization redesigns workflows, roles, controls, and system usage to improve speed, accuracy, cost efficiency, and visibility.

Does SBS develop SOPs?

Yes. SBS creates practical SOPs, governance models, approval matrices, and documentation that support consistent execution.

Can process optimization reduce operating costs?

Yes. By removing duplicate steps, improving controls, and automating manual work, SBS helps businesses reduce avoidable effort and cost.
